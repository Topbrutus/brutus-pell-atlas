## 8. Future bridges

These are research directions, not established consequences of the current atlas:

- Pell / Lucas divisibility sequences;
- Diophantine equations;
- quartic models;
- elliptic-curve reductions;
- graph and hypergraph representations of rank covers;
- systematic classification of square-rank fibers;
- systematic classification of mirror hosts.

## Atlas principle

Every connection should carry one of four labels:

`VERIFIED_COMPUTATION` — recomputed exactly by this repository.

`DERIVED_RELATION` — follows from declared mathematical assumptions.

`KNOWN_THEORY` — standard result requiring external citation.

`RESEARCH_CANDIDATE` — hypothesis or classification awaiting proof/antériorité.
